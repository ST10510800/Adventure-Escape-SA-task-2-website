ABOUT ADVENTURE ESCAPE SA

OUR MISSION:

A local small to mid-size enterprise (SME) aims to develop a web page and mobile app for advertising their services, processing booking requests, and providing quotes for adventure activities. Founded by Liam Daniels, an outdoor adventure and eco-tourism enthusiast, Adventure Escape SA caters to families, tourists, schools, and corporate groups by offering diverse guided outdoor experiences that promote teamwork, fitness, and a love of nature.
